Championship & Points System

Understand the dual championships and simulate finishing point allocations in real-time.

WDC Individual

World Drivers' Championship (WDC)

Awarded to the driver who accumulates the highest total points across all Grand Prix events in a season. Drivers compete directly even against their own teammates driving identical machinery.

Key Steward Note: Driver points remain tied to the driver even if they switch teams mid-season.
WCC Team Battle

World Constructors' Championship (WCC)

Combines the total points scored by BOTH drivers representing the team. WCC standings directly determine team prize money distribution worth tens of millions of dollars.

Key Steward Note: If a driver is disqualified (DSQ), their points for that race are wiped from WCC totals too.

Safety Car (SC) vs Virtual Safety Car (VSC)

SC (Safety Car)

Physical Safety Car Deployed

Deploys actual vehicle (Mercedes-AMG/Aston Martin) onto track to bunch up the field during serious hazards.

  • All overtaking prohibited. Pace set by SC driver.
  • Enables "Cheap Pit Stop" (saves ~10-12 seconds compared to normal race speed pit stop).
  • Time gaps between cars compressed to zero at restart.
VSC (Virtual Safety Car)

Electronic Speed Limitation

Used for minor incidents requiring marshals on track for quick cleanup without physical car.

  • All drivers must reduce speed by ~30% and match Target Delta Time on steering wheel.
  • Existing time gaps between drivers are preserved.
  • Can end abruptly; drivers must react instantly to green flag.

MODULE 03

Pit Lane Rules & Tyre Strategy

Mandatory pit stops, pit speed limits, and setup freezing under Parc Fermé.

Dry Tyre Compound Mandate

In dry weather conditions, every finishing driver **MUST use at least two different dry slick tyre compounds** during the race (e.g. Soft + Medium, or Medium + Hard).

💡 If rain occurs and Intermediate or Wet tyres are used, this two-compound rule is waived.

Pit Speed & Unsafe Release

Pit Lane Speed limit is strictly enforced at **80 km/h** (reduced to **60 km/h** at tight tracks like Monaco). Speeding results in time penalties.

**Unsafe Release:** Releasing a car into the path of an oncoming vehicle in pit lane triggers immediate driver/team penalties.

⚡ Pit Stop Speed: Top crews complete a 4-wheel tire change in under **2.0 seconds**!

Parc Fermé Setup Freeze

From the moment cars leave the garage for Qualifying, they enter **Parc Fermé**. Major mechanical setups, suspension tuning, or major component swaps are strictly frozen.

⚠️ Violation Penalty: Unauthorized setup changes force the car to start from the **Pit Lane**.

Official Stewards Penalty Directory

5-Second Time Penalty

Added to driver's total race time or served during their next pit stop (pit crew must stand stationary for 5s before touching the car).

10-Second Time Penalty

Imposed for severe incidents (e.g. causing a collision or dangerous moving under braking).

Drive-Through Penalty

Driver must enter pit lane, observe the 80 km/h speed limit, and re-join WITHOUT stopping for tires. Costs ~20–25s.

10-Second Stop & Go

Driver enters pit lane, stops in penalty box for 10 full seconds without crew working on the car, then resumes.

Grid Position Penalty

Applied to the driver's starting position for the next race (e.g., 3-grid, 5-grid, or 10-grid drop for engine component limits or crashes).

Super Licence Penalty Points

Dangerous driving incurs 1–3 points on Super Licence. Reaching **12 points within a rolling 12-month period triggers an automatic 1-RACE BAN**.
